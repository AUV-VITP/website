Team AUV · VIT Pune

Underwater vehicles, engineered end to end

A student team building autonomous underwater vehicles. Mechanical design, embedded systems, controls and perception, taken from CAD through simulation to the tank.

See our work auv@vit.edu

What we do

Design, build and validate autonomous vehicles

Design and build

AUVs for RoboSub and related challenges, from hull form and thruster layout to pressure housings and mass budget.

Sense and decide

Sensing, navigation and onboard autonomy running on embedded flight hardware, with real sonar in the loop.

Validate early

Hardware and software proven in simulation and on the bench before anything goes into a tank.

  • Embedded systems
